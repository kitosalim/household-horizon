2. Retirement plan

Partner

Spending is drawn from super once you are both retired. Until then, your remaining income is assumed to cover living costs.

Where your balance should be at each age

All dollar figures are in today’s money. “Balance needed” is what you should hold at that age to reach your retirement target if you keep up your current contributions. Defaults such as the spending figures and 12% employer rate are approximate, so check the latest ASFA Retirement Standard and ATO rates. Some super funds offer interest-free or ethically screened options, so compare funds and confirm suitability with your own adviser.
