The short version

The calculator runs entirely in your web browser. The figures you type in (income, expenses, balances and so on) are not sent to us, and we do not ask you to create an account.

Information you enter

  • Everything you enter is processed on your own device.
  • If you press Save, your entries are stored in your browser's local storage on that device only. We cannot see them.
  • If you press Save to device or Download PDF, a file containing the details you entered is created on your device. Keep it somewhere safe, because anyone who has the file can read it.
  • You can delete saved profiles with the Delete button, or by clearing your browser's site data.
